## What I work on

- **Computer Vision & Deep Learning** — object detection, image classification, and recommendation systems using YOLO and neural networks.
- **Systems Development** — full-cycle software engineering, from requirements gathering to deployment, with a focus on automation and data-driven decision making.
- **Applied AI for Social Impact** — solutions for public service monitoring and agricultural pest control.
- **Scientific Computing** — integration of computational intelligence with real-world problems in the public and agribusiness sectors.

## Background

I am an undergraduate student in Information Systems at the Federal Institute of Espírito Santo (IFES), with a strong focus on applied research in computer vision, artificial intelligence, and systems development. I have participated in technological innovation projects in partnership with the Court of Justice of Espírito Santo (VEPEMA) and the strawberry production sector in Santa Maria de Jetibá — one of the main strawberry-producing regions in Brazil. My experience includes data modeling, API architecture, software engineering, and scientific computing. I have presented my work at national exhibitions, including the National Week of Professional and Technological Education and the National Week of Science and Technology, and have supervised scientific initiation projects funded by FAPES.
